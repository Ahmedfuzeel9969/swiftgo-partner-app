Privacy Policy

SwiftGo (ride-hailing web apps for customers, drivers/partners, fleet owners, and operators).

What we collect

  • Account identifiers (name, email, phone when provided)
  • Location data while using map / ride matching features
  • Ride, offer, wallet, and settlement records
  • Driver KYC / identity documents when you apply as a driver
  • Support messages and complaint reports you submit

Why we use it

  • Provide matching, booking, navigation assistance, and customer support
  • Prevent fraud, enforce account safety, and settle fares
  • Meet bookkeeping and dispute requirements

Sharing

We share trip details with the assigned driver/partner and, when required, authorized operators. We use Firebase/Google infrastructure. We do not sell personal data in this draft product posture.

Retention

Profile and login access can be disabled on account deletion request. Financial ledger, settlement, and audit records are retained where required for disputes, taxation, or legal obligations. Exact retention periods require owner/legal approval (see Legal Review Items).
