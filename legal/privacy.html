Privacy Policy

SwiftGo (ride-hailing web apps for customers, drivers/partners, fleet owners, and operators).

What we collect

  • Account identifiers (name, email, phone when provided)
  • Location data while using map / ride matching features
  • Ride, offer, wallet, and settlement records
  • Driver KYC / identity documents when you apply as a driver
  • Support messages and complaint reports you submit

Why we use it

  • Provide matching, booking, navigation assistance, and customer support
  • Prevent fraud, enforce account safety, and settle fares
  • Meet bookkeeping and dispute requirements

Sharing

We share trip details with the assigned driver/partner and authorized operators according to their role. We use Firebase/Google infrastructure. Live location uses an authenticated peer connection first and an administrator-controlled Firebase fallback. Peer networking can expose network addresses to the other participant and, when enabled, relay providers. Map tiles, route requests and address searches contact configured external services; the current preview uses OpenStreetMap, OSRM and Nominatim services. These providers can receive your network address and the map area, route endpoints or search text needed for the request.

Pasted short map links are no longer forwarded to public CORS proxies. Copy a full coordinate link, enter an address, or select a map pin instead. This does not eliminate the external services required by map tiles, route calculation or address search.

Retention

A deletion request blocks application access and requests login disable/revocation. A failed operation is recorded for retry; a request is not confirmation that data has been erased. Requests during an active ride or a current/scheduled legacy booking are refused until resolved. The owner-approved policy uses a 30-day deadline for ordinary personal-data cleanup after closure, one calendar year after closure for approved identity records, and 90 days after rejection or withdrawal for unsuccessful identity applications. Unfinished applications are treated as withdrawn on account closure. Outstanding balances, vehicle obligations, complaints and legal holds can prevent disposal; the operator must resolve and record those conditions.

Financial records and necessary settlement evidence are retained for at least ten financial years after the relevant financial-year end. This business policy anticipates later company registration; it does not assert that the operator is already incorporated. The current calendar assumes a 30 June year end in Pakistan and must be checked against the registered entity's actual accounting year before activation. After the minimum, financial records require a documented review rather than automatic destruction. Financial identifiers, booked route evidence and required audit records are not included in ordinary profile erasure.

Temporary location and connection records have server-controlled expiry metadata. Location-report retention still follows the super administrator's configured window. Separately gated, bounded workflows can remove profile/contact copies and login credentials, and eligible identity records with their exact stored images. Expiry fields alone do not prove removal. Live activation, monitoring of deadlines, legacy records without reliable dates or file references, provider object versions/soft-delete copies, exports and backups require separate verification. These workflows do not certify global physical erasure. The operator's jurisdiction, legal basis, registration details and contact address still require appropriate legal review before release.
