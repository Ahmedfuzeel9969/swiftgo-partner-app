How SwiftGo uses your data

Location

We ask for location permission so the map can show your position, set pickup points, match nearby drivers using geo cells / distance rings, and help navigation during an active trip. You can deny permission; some features will fall back to a default map center and may be less accurate.

KYC and documents

Driver applications may collect name, CNIC, license, vehicle details, and a selfie or document photos. These are used to verify partners before they go online. Documents are stored in private storage paths and are not meant for public browsing.

Wallet and settlement records

Partner wallet balances, recharge requests, ride fares, and settlement ledgers are maintained as trusted server records. They support payouts, dispute resolution, and financial accountability. When you delete your account, these ledger and audit records are not silently erased.

Camera / microphone

Camera may be requested for driver selfie capture during onboarding. Microphone is not required for core booking. We explain the purpose in-app before requesting access.
